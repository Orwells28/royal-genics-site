About Royal Genics

BUILT TO SOLVE HARD PROBLEMS.

Systems thinking / accountable delivery

BUILT THROUGH EXPERIENCE.

I didn’t arrive in technology through a traditional path. I grew up in a difficult environment without many of the advantages or support systems that make a career path straightforward. When I was younger, I made decisions that eventually brought legal consequences and left me trying to rebuild my life with a record.

That reality forced me to become resourceful. Traditional doors were not always open, so I learned to find other doors. I explored different industries, learned new skills, worked through unfamiliar problems, and became comfortable entering environments where I had to figure things out quickly.

Over time, that experience developed into one of my strongest professional abilities: solving complex problems. Technology was always the field that interested me most. Breaking into it was difficult, but eventually I did. Once there, I found an industry that rewarded curiosity, adaptability, critical thinking, persistence, systems thinking, and the ability to connect seemingly unrelated pieces into a working solution.

Today, those experiences shape how I approach Royal Genics. I don’t look at a requirement and ask only, “Do we already offer this service?” I ask: What is the objective? What resources does it require? What technology can improve it? Who needs to be involved? What risks need to be managed? And how do we put those pieces together into something that works?

“Complexity doesn’t intimidate me. Complexity is usually where I do my best work.”

My professional career has expanded across technology, enterprise systems, automation, agile delivery, and AI-assisted workflows. I have worked with ServiceNow environments, technical operations, systems processes, and enterprise technology while earning multiple ServiceNow certifications and professional certifications related to modern service and delivery methodologies.

More recently, I have heavily incorporated AI orchestration into my workflows—coordinating AI tools, automation, software, data, and human decision-making to reduce repetitive work, accelerate research and development, improve documentation, and execute complex workflows with greater speed and accuracy.

AI is not the replacement for judgment. It is leverage. Royal Genics uses that leverage to move faster while maintaining human accountability for what gets delivered.
