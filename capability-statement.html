Who We Are

Royal Genics LLC is a contract procurement company serving both public and private sector clients. We identify, bid, win, and manage contracts end to end — and we build the automation and workflow systems that run them. Where a requirement calls for specialist field capacity, we deliver it through a vetted partner network under our own management and accountability, so the client keeps a single point of contact.

Core Competencies
Self-performed

Contract Capture & Program Management

We run the full contracting lifecycle in house.

  • Opportunity identification, qualification, and pipeline tracking
  • Bid and proposal development, including compliance review against solicitation instructions
  • Subcontractor sourcing, vetting, and teaming arrangements
  • Deadline, deliverable, and reporting management through period of performance
  • Single point of accountability to the contracting officer
Self-performed

Workflow Automation & Agentic Systems

We build and operate the systems we sell — this is our own engineering work, not brokered.

  • Business process and workflow automation
  • Data infrastructure, tracking databases, and pipeline systems
  • Automated monitoring, alerting, and reporting
  • Agentic / AI-assisted process design and integration
  • Outreach, intake, and administrative automation
Delivered through vetted partner network

Managed Service Delivery

Field services delivered by qualified partner firms, contracted and managed by Royal Genics. We hold the contract, manage performance, and remain accountable for delivery.

  • Custodial and janitorial services
  • Courier, medical specimen, and logistics services
  • Grounds maintenance and landscaping
  • Facilities and base operations support
Differentiators

Single point of accountability

One contract, one responsible party. We manage the partners so the client does not have to.

We run the systems we sell

Our automation capability is self-performed and used daily on our own operations, not resold.

Pre-qualified partner bench

Partners are sourced and vetted ahead of award, so capacity is confirmed before we bid.

Public and private sector

Federal, state, local, and commercial. We are not limited to one contracting channel.
